Our methodology

A guided journey — not a one-time test.

Most career tests hand a student a label. We walk them through five stages, from understanding who they are to a plan they can act on.

1

DISCOVER

Interactive, game-based activities reveal how the student thinks, what they enjoy and how they work.

2

UNDERSTAND

Results come together into one clear profile of interests, personality, aptitude and values.

3

EXPLORE

Matched career worlds show what each path really involves — day-to-day work, skills, growth.

4

DECIDE

Students, parents and counsellors weigh options together using evidence, not guesswork.

5

PLAN

A concrete roadmap: subject stream, courses, colleges, entrance exams and next steps.

Founder's story
“I spent 17 years understanding people in the corporate world. I now want to help young people understand themselves before they enter it.”

Across nearly two decades of hiring, developing and coaching professionals, I kept meeting talented people who had drifted into careers that didn't fit them — simply because nobody helped them understand themselves at 15 or 16.

myCareerDNA brings the same tools organisations use to understand people to students, parents and schools — in a form that's engaging for young people and meaningful for their families.
