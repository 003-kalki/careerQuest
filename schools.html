Frequently asked questions

Which grades is it designed for?

Grades 8 to 12, with the biggest impact before stream selection (grade 10) and before college applications (grades 11–12).

Do parents need to pay?

In the school programme, access is provided through the school. Parents receive their child's profile as part of it.

How much time does it take from the timetable?

Students complete the journey in scheduled sessions [duration to confirm]; it can also be done at home.

What support do counsellors get?

An orientation session, the dashboard, and access to our experts for interpreting complex profiles.
