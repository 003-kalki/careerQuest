Questions parents ask

Frequently asked questions

What age is this suitable for?

myCareerDNA is designed for students in grades 8 to 12 — ideally before stream selection in grade 10, and again before college decisions.

Is this just another online aptitude test?

No. It measures five dimensions through interactive experiences, connects them to real career pathways and courses, and includes human expert interpretation.

Will it tell my child exactly what to become?

It won't hand out a single label. It shows the career worlds that fit your child best and why — so your family can make an informed decision together.

How is my child's data protected?

Student data is kept private and secure, is never sold, and is shared only with the parent and — for school programmes — the school's counsellor.

How long does it take?
